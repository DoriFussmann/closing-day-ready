# Design Guide

## Brand Identity

- **Brand Name:** Closing Day Ready
- **Brand Feel:** Trustworthy, calm, friendly, professional, modern, helpful, organized, reassuring
- **Mission:** Become the most trusted educational resource for homebuyers by simplifying the process into a clear journey.
- **Brand Values:** Clarity over complexity,Education before promotion,Accuracy and trust,Calm confidence,Transparency,Accessibility,Practical guidance
- **Emotional Goal:** Visitors should feel informed, prepared, and confident, thinking 'I finally understand what happens next.'
- **Brand References:** Apple Support, NerdWallet, Stripe Documentation, Wirecutter, Notion Help Center

## Typography

- **Heading Font:** Inter (Light 300, SemiBold 600 only when needed)
- **Body Font:** Inter (Light 300 or Regular 400)
- **Mono Font:** _(not set)_
- **Type Feel:** Elegant, not loud; avoid heavy or bold typography
- **Type Scale:** H1 44-52px, H2 34px, H3 28px, H4 22px, Body 18px, Small 15-16px
- **Line Height:** H1 1.15, Body 1.7
- **Font Weights:** Light 300, Regular 400, SemiBold 600 (sparingly); avoid Bold 700+

- **Breakpoints:** _(not set)_
