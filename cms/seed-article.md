Technical SEO is not only about rankings. It is also about making every claim easy for crawlers and answer engines to parse, attribute, and cite.

## Structured data and authorship

When an article declares its author as a real `Person` with credentials and profile links, readers and machines can evaluate expertise. Pair that with `BlogPosting` JSON-LD, breadcrumbs, and optional FAQ schema.

## Internal links and sources

Distinct related-link and source blocks keep navigation honest. Internal links should point at real routes; external links should cite independent material rather than the site itself.
